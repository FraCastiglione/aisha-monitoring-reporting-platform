# AISHA platform audit — 1 October 2026

The changes were checked in local preview before the user-authorised GitHub publication. This audit describes the source and functional checks performed for that release.

The audit covers the AISHA application and its supporting coordinator tools. The separate AI-SECRETT application under `ai-secrett/` was left unchanged.

## Repairs and requested updates

- Gantt markers are contained within calendar cells and appear beneath opaque sticky labels. WP10 is protected like the other Work Packages. Sticky headers sit above row labels when vertically scrolling an expanded Gantt.
- Technical Progress Report preparation appears as purple upper and lower rails in the review windows. Green Work Package and blue Task activity bars remain intact. Eligibility follows overlap with the covered period: M1–18, M19–36 or M37–48. Header review labels remain M19–20, M37–38 and M49–50. No extra Task or preparation row is added.
- Removed the obsolete instruction below the Gantt.
- The selected-month summary fills its section. Selected timeline months use the summary's existing pale green. Save Your Progress keeps its layout and wording.
- Exact KPI source tables and objectives are accessible in the dashboard and report builder. Links connect source rows, reportable fields and related Tasks.
- Every partner can record its own KPI values. Suggested contributors are shown first; other relevant indicators remain available. Communication reporting contains communication, dissemination, outreach and policy-engagement measures; programme enrolment and completion fields remain in the general report.
- An activity reference distinguishes programmes, cohorts, campaigns and outputs. Multiple contributions to one KPI are retained separately. Editing and importing use both indicator ID and activity reference.
- Counts, decimals, percentages and statuses have appropriate validation. Percentage calculation counts are checked when supplied. Growth can exceed 100% and can be negative. Measurement scales and calculation sources are required for percentages and scores.
- Unsaved KPI editor fields are cleared when changing partner or report type. Communication saves also enforce the communication indicator list.
- Communication drafts and reports now have a restoration route. New communication PDFs embed the structured answers, like Task PDFs. A PDF import restored the right partner, reporting period, event, article links, social counts, Unknown reach and KPI claim in preview.
- Draft PDFs no longer depend on a previously selected Task timeline. Unanswered communication Yes/No fields remain unanswered in the readable draft. Unknown reach is shown as Unknown during review.
- KPI-only drafts saved before months are chosen can be imported into a current report without replacing its timeline or Task entries.
- Private coordinator intake accepts current Task schema 2.4 and communication schema 1.2, including additional-only and KPI-only reports. Claims and supporting narratives are retained in a private review companion. Public KPI actuals remain unchanged until reviewed.
- Repaired the older assignment audit, which still assumed associated partners had only formal Annex 1 routes. It now independently checks the confirmed Attachment 6 commitments without changing the reporting assignments.
- Disjoint partial reporting periods within the same month are accepted. Actual day overlaps still block duplicate intake.
- The report builder now leads with saved KPI values and a compact indicator finder. Partners can search the complete source-backed catalogue, filter by group, save favourites, and reveal more results in short batches. Suggested indicators are shown first, with the Agreement wording kept in an optional reference library.
- The dashboard has matching indicator search, group filtering and favourites. Exact name matches are listed first; no partner claim becomes a public actual through this interface.
- Percentage fields that explicitly require a numerator and denominator now request both values and check their calculation. Other percentage and score fields still require their measurement basis or scale.
- The private coordinator intake can produce a claim-by-claim KPI review queue with evidence references, source warnings, decisions and shared activity-reference signals. Decisions remain manual, and the queue is outside the public repository.
- The Gantt contains the 20–21 May 2026 kickoff at La Sapienza facilities, Rome, and the 18–19 November 2026 consortium meeting in Brussels. Meetings appear in the month headers and calendar agenda, without a vertical event line through activity rows.

## Clarifications and proposed next improvements

1. Approve the proposed KPI contributors and decide who verifies each measure, with IF.E/TRS responsible for project website and social analytics.
2. Resolve source differences before agreeing one target for dashboard progress: scheme funding (€400,000 total versus EUR500,000 per scheme), evaluation framework M6 versus M12, annual versus aggregate women-beneficiary targets, campaign conversion 3% versus 2–5%, and DSJP M6 milestone versus M12 communication KPI wording. Keep attendance distinct from aggregate reach. Dissemination phase labels in the narrative differ from formal reporting periods.
3. The new search and favourites reduce browsing across the expanded catalogue; continue testing with real partners to refine the suggested ordering.
4. Move from the current free-text activity reference to governed programme/cohort/campaign IDs, country/gender breakdowns and shared output IDs once those identifiers are agreed. Do not automatically sum audiences, people, overlapping activities, account totals or rates.
5. The new private coordinator review queue holds KPI decisions and source warnings. Human verification of evidence, correction requests and approved aggregation rules are still needed; the tool cannot access Nextcloud.
6. After real monitoring rounds are defined, show each partner's requested scope, deadline and remaining coverage. Missing entries alone do not prove a partner is late.
7. Keep repeatable regression checks for the main import, export, timeline and partner-switch journeys on subsequent releases.

Generated AISHA PDFs with embedded report data and their matching JSON backups can be mapped reliably by identity and selected entries. Older or arbitrary PDFs without that data require their matching JSON or manual transcription. No OCR mapping is presented as error-free.
