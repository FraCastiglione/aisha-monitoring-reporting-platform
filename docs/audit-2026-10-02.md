# Update audit · 2 October 2026

## Changes checked

- The partner report now offers KPI suggestions from entered narrative. A suggestion is reviewable and can be dismissed; a measured value is never saved without the partner completing the KPI form and evidence declaration. The first suggested list is shorter and prioritises indicators connected to assigned Tasks. Social-post-count searches direct users to the structured Communication & Dissemination check-in.
- A shared activity code travels with a KPI claim, a communication event, editable drafts and generated PDFs. A repeated code within one indicator and report is caught before saving. The private coordinator review queue groups repeated codes across partners and indicators, while maintaining human evidence and overlap checks. It does not update dashboard actuals.
- The seven Agreement source differences open to show their separate original passages and page references. Their interpretation remains pending. The public snapshot has empty monitoring-round and shared-activity registries until the coordinator supplies confirmed entries.
- Private coordinator files and partner submissions remain outside the public Git repository.

- Automated checks covered a factual candidate count, a planned future value that must not be prefilled, an invalid shared code, stable activity grouping across different labels, and a cross-partner overlap in the private queue.

## Limits

- The text suggestions are deliberately conservative and cover selected recognisable indicators. They can miss valid KPI contributions or suggest a related but inapplicable indicator; they are prompts for partner review, not extraction of certified actuals.
- Shared activity codes help identify possible overlap. They do not establish whether two values measure the same people or should be summed. A coordinator must examine the evidence and measurement population.
- The private evidence-review page was checked statically, but browser security disallowed opening its local `file:` URL for interactive testing. The queue generator and decision guards were tested in code. No real partner submission or evidence has been reviewed yet.
- Monitoring-round dates, actual messages, shared activity registry entries, report receipts and KPI actuals have not been supplied; they remain unfilled.
