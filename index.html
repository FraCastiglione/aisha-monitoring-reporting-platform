PARTNER GUIDE

How to report

The AISHA website helps you prepare files. The coordination team will specify where each monitoring round’s report should be sent.

  1. 01

    Prepare your report

    Choose your organisation and report type, then answer for your organisation’s contribution during the relevant period.

  2. 02

    Review and download

    Check your details and answers, then retain both the readable PDF and the structured JSON file.

  3. 03

    Share the report files

    Send both files through the channel specified by the AISHA coordination team and confirm receipt there. This preview does not send them.

About this preview

The beneficiary, associated partner, Work Package and Task catalogue comes from the Grant Agreement. Reporting periods and the two-week communication check-in are coordinator workflow choices; the coordination team should confirm each monitoring round before official use.
