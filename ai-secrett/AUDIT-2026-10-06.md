## Palette audit

Violet is the brand and selection accent. Body text and labels use dark slate. Task activity and successful completion use teal/green, report preparation uses blue, internal drafts use amber, official deadlines use red, and unrecorded/no-work states use neutral slate. Leader, participant and contributor badges have distinct text and backgrounds. Legend shapes and written labels supplement colours.

The browser audit found and repaired faint Gantt month numbers, calendar weekdays, source captions, Work Package output captions and reporting labels. Rechecked visible text against solid backgrounds in all seven dashboard sections: no remaining measured contrast below 4.5:1 for normal text or 3:1 for large text in that check. The gradient hero was visually inspected separately; this is not a full accessibility certification.
